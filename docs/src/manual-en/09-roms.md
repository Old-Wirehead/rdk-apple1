# Installing a ROM or the original Integer BASIC

<p class="lead">Nothing else is required. The Woz Monitor, the ACI cassette ROM and RDK G2 Basic are already in the program. This page is for anyone who wants Apple’s Integer BASIC, or another compatible ROM, from an outside source.</p>

The kit **does not redistribute any proprietary ROM file**. The Apple-1, the 1976 Integer BASIC and the commercial tapes stay with their rights holders. The links below point at sites that already exist: you fetch your own copy there. The kit does not contain it.

## What is already built in

- **Woz Monitor**, at `$FF00`.
- **ACI cassette ROM**, at `$C100`.
- **RDK G2 Basic**, a free BASIC compatible with Integer BASIC, loaded by the **BASIC** button at `$E000`.

## Original Integer BASIC

1. Get your own copy, for example the `BASIC.wav` tape of the “Apple1Cassettes” collection on archive.org: <https://archive.org/details/Apple1Cassettes>.
2. Put the file in your library, or keep it wherever you like.
3. Open **⚙ Preferences → Advanced → BASIC**, choose **original Integer BASIC**, then **📂 Choose…** and point at the file (a `.wav`, a `.txt`, or a 4,096-byte `.bin`).
4. The **BASIC** button then loads that file. **Forget** goes back to the built-in G2 Basic.

The Apple-1 community meets at <https://www.applefritter.com/>. The kit downloads nothing from these sites: the link is only a pointer.
